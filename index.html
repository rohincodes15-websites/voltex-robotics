We started VOLTEX in 2026 because we love building things. Now we're a team of eight, working on one robot together.

Every season, FTC gives teams a new game. We have a few months to design a robot, build it, program it, and get it ready for competition. We keep track of everything we try in our engineering notebook.

We're split into three groups: hardware, software, and outreach. Everyone has a real job, and everyone gets a say.

This season

This is our first year, so a lot of it is about learning. We're figuring out how to build a robot that works every time, not just once. We test early, break things, fix them, and try again.

Our goal

We want to do well at our first competitions and qualify for the next level. Just as much, we want to build a team that lasts, so new members can keep it going after we graduate.

How we work

We plan together, split up the work, and check in on each other's progress. When something breaks, we figure out why before we fix it.

Outside the robot

We also run outreach, like showing younger students what robotics is about and sharing our progress on Instagram. It's a big part of what FTC is about.
